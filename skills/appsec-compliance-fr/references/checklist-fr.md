# Checklist: sites and apps offered in France

Each line: what is required, whether it is mandatory, the source to cite. Keep only what applies. Sources are given so the user and their adviser can verify them; laws change.

## Identity of the publisher

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mentions légales: publisher identity (company name, legal form, share capital, registered office, RCS or RNE number, VAT number, phone, e-mail), publication director, host (name, address, phone). An individual publishing without professional activity may stay anonymous publicly but must identify the host | Mandatory | LCEN (loi 2004-575) art. 6 III; the numbering was changed by loi 2024-449 (SREN): cite the current article |

## Personal data (GDPR / RGPD, loi Informatique et Libertés)

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy notice: controller and contact, purposes and legal basis for each, recipients and processors, transfers outside the EU and their safeguards, retention per data type, rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, post-mortem directives in France), right to complain to the CNIL | Mandatory as soon as personal data is collected | GDPR art. 13 and 14; loi I&L art. 85 (post-mortem directives) |
| Processor contracts with every provider handling personal data (hosting, database, e-mail, payment, analytics, LLM APIs) | Mandatory | GDPR art. 28 |
| Transfers outside the EU: adequacy decision (e.g. EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified US companies) or standard contractual clauses | Mandatory when data leaves the EU | GDPR chapter V |
| Record of processing activities | Mandatory in practice (exemption for small organisations is narrow) | GDPR art. 30 |
| Data minimisation and retention periods actually applied in the product | Mandatory | GDPR art. 5 |
| Rights in practice: account deletion and data export reachable without friction | Mandatory (rights must be exercisable) | GDPR art. 12, 15 to 21 |
| Security of processing, breach notification to the CNIL within 72 hours when required, breach register | Mandatory | GDPR art. 32, 33, 34 |
| Minors: consent of a parent below 15 for services based on consent | Mandatory when relevant | loi I&L art. 45 |
| DPIA for high-risk processing (large-scale sensitive data, systematic monitoring) | Mandatory when relevant | GDPR art. 35, CNIL lists |

## Cookies and trackers

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Consent before any non-essential cookie or tracker (analytics, advertising, social widgets, some fonts and maps loaded from third parties); "Refuser" as easy as "Accepter"; consent proof; ability to withdraw | Mandatory when such trackers exist | loi I&L art. 82; CNIL guidelines and recommendation of 17 September 2020 |
| No banner needed when only strictly necessary cookies are used (session, cart, security, load balancing, consent storage) or audience measurement configured within the CNIL exemption | Rule | Same |
| Cookie information (in the privacy notice or a dedicated page) | Mandatory when cookies exist | Same |

## Selling to consumers at a distance

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-contractual information and terms of sale (CGV): seller identity, essential characteristics, total price including taxes and delivery costs, payment, delivery date, withdrawal, guarantees, mediator | Mandatory | Code de la consommation L111-1, L221-5 |
| Order button worded as an obligation to pay ("Commander avec obligation de paiement" or equivalent) | Mandatory | C. conso L221-14 |
| Right of withdrawal: 14 days, model withdrawal form, refund within 14 days, listed exceptions | Mandatory | C. conso L221-18 and following, annex to R221-1 |
| Withdrawal function on the website for contracts concluded online | Mandatory for online contracts from 19 June 2026 (EU rule; check the French transposition) | Directive (EU) 2023/2673 |
| Legal guarantees: conformity (2 years) and hidden defects, with the information box required since 2022 | Mandatory | C. conso L217-3 and following; Code civil 1641; décret 2022-946 |
| Consumer mediator: membership and contact details given to consumers | Mandatory | C. conso L612-1, L616-1 |
| Order confirmation on a durable medium (e-mail) | Mandatory | C. conso L221-13; Code civil 1127-2 |
| Prices shown including VAT for consumers | Mandatory | C. conso L112-1 |
| French language for consumer information | Mandatory | Loi Toubon (94-665); C. conso L111-1 |

## User content and online reviews

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Online reviews: say whether and how reviews are checked, how they are published and ranked, and whether they may be removed | Mandatory when reviews are shown | C. conso L111-7-2, D111-16 and following |
| Notice-and-action to report illegal content | Mandatory for hosting services | DSA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065) art. 16 |
| Terms of use (CGU) for accounts and user content | Recommended | Contract law |

## AI features

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Users told they are interacting with an AI system, unless obvious | Mandatory from 2 August 2026 | AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) art. 50 |
| Privacy notice covers data sent to the AI provider, its location and retention | Mandatory | GDPR art. 13, chapter V |

## Accessibility

| Item | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| E-commerce and other listed services must be accessible since 28 June 2025, with accessibility information; micro-enterprises providing services (under 10 staff and 2 M€ turnover or balance sheet) are exempt | Mandatory unless exempt | European Accessibility Act, Directive (EU) 2019/882; loi 2023-171 |
| Public bodies and large companies: RGAA conformity and an accessibility statement | Mandatory for them | Loi 2005-102 art. 47; RGAA 4.1 |
| WCAG 2.1 AA as the technical target | Recommended for everyone | WCAG 2.1 |
